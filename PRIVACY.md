# Privacy policy of the SGO plugins for AI assistants

Effective date: October 7, 2026

This policy covers the plugins published in this repository, such as the Mistika Workflows plugin, in every app and directory where they are distributed. They are published by SGO (Soluciones Graficas por Ordenador SL, https://www.sgo.es).

## What the plugins are

The plugins contain instructions only (skills) that an AI assistant reads. They contain no code that runs when they are installed, no server and no analytics, and SGO operates no service behind them.

## Data the plugins collect

None. The plugins do not collect, receive, store or share personal data or any other data:

- **Categories of personal data collected**: none.
- **Purposes of use**: none, because no data is collected.
- **Recipients**: none. SGO receives no data through the plugins.
- **Retention**: none. SGO stores no data from the plugins, so there is nothing to keep or delete.
- **Your controls**: you can disable or uninstall a plugin at any time in your AI app.

## Data handled by others when you use a plugin

- **Your AI app.** Your conversations, including what the assistant reads and does while it follows a plugin, are processed by the provider of the AI app you use, under that provider's privacy policy.
- **Mistika Workflows.** The plugins help the assistant use Mistika Workflows, which runs on your own computer and processes your media there. The assistant talks to it through its MCP server, also on your computer. Mistika Workflows sends data to other services only when a workflow you build or approve uses them, such as a cloud storage, delivery or AI service, under the terms of those services.
- **SGO websites and products.** Downloading Mistika Workflows from sgo.es, activating its license and contacting SGO support are covered by the [SGO privacy policy](https://www.sgo.es/privacy-policy-statement/) and the [Mistika Workflows license agreement](https://www.sgo.es/eula/).

## Changes

If the plugins change in a way that affects your data, this policy will be updated and its effective date changed.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, contact [SGO support](https://support.sgo.es).
